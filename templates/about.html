About Dr. P.V. Varadarajan

In 1960, Dr. P.V. Varadarajan researched on Siddha Medicines and succeeded in earning his R.I.M.P Doctorate from the Tamil Nadu Government.

He was continuously doing his research in the Sarasaparilla (Nannari) extract. Through dedicated research, he discovered that Nannari is a delicious herbal root whose pleasant extract delivers incredible taste along with rich medicinal benefits.

In 1961, he founded this venture along with his two brothers, P.V. Muthusamy and R.V. Ramachandran, under the name "Janatha". Due to uncompromised product quality, hard work, matchless efforts, sincerity, and God’s grace, Janatha has grown to become one of the largest suppliers of Nannari Syrup, Fruit Juices, and authentic food products across Tamil Nadu and throughout the country.
